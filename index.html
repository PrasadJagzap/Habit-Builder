The Science of Habits

Built on three timeless principles

Every feature in Habit-Builder traces back to ideas proven by behavioral science and made famous by James Clear.

01
The 1% Rule
Improving by just 1% each day leads to being 37× better by the end of the year. Tiny gains don't feel significant in the moment — but they compound into something remarkable.
37× better in one year
02
Identity-Based Habits
Every action you take is a vote for the type of person you wish to become. The goal is not to run a marathon — it is to become a runner. Habits shape identity; identity shapes destiny.
Every check-in is a vote
03
The Habit Loop
All habits follow a four-stage loop: Cue → Craving → Response → Reward. Understanding this loop is the key to building new habits and breaking old ones intentionally.
Cue → Craving → Response → Reward
